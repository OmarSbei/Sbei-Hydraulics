Cookie Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how S&S Hydraulics uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website.

01. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and provide tracking information to site owners.

02. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies primarily to save your preferences, such as your Light/Dark mode theme selection, and to understand how visitors interact with our site so we can improve the user interface.

03. Manage Your Cookie Preferences

You can customize your cookie settings below at any time. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for basic site functionality.

Essential Cookies Required for core website security, session management, and basic functionality.
Preference Cookies Allows the website to remember choices you make (such as your UI theme preference).
Analytics Cookies Helps us understand how visitors interact with the site via Google Search Console and analytics tools.

04. Browser Controls

You also have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies through your web browser controls. If you choose to reject cookies globally, some site functionality may be restricted.
