FAQ

What is the difference between a hydraulic pump and a hydraulic motor?
A hydraulic pump creates hydraulic flow, while a hydraulic motor converts hydraulic flow into rotary mechanical motion.
How do I choose the correct pump or motor?
Selection depends on pressure, flow rate, speed, torque, and the specific application requirements.
Do you supply replacement pumps and motors?
Yes. We provide complete replacement units as well as technical assistance for selecting compatible hydraulic components.
