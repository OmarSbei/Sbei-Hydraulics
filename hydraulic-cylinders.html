Overview

Hydraulic cylinders convert hydraulic pressure into linear force and motion.

S&S Hydraulics supplies high-quality hydraulic cylinders for industrial, construction, agricultural and mobile equipment.

Hydraulic Cylinder Types

Cylinders & servocylinders

Square or round heads with tie-rods or counterflanges. Low friction seals and built-in position transducer for high precision motion control. Safety executions integrate IEC 61508 certified transducers and/or rod locking systems.

View Types

Servoactuators

Smart and compact units that integrate servocylinders and axis control valves to fully manage the motion cycle.

FAQ

What is a hydraulic cylinder?
It converts hydraulic energy into linear mechanical motion.
How do I choose the correct cylinder?
Based on bore size, stroke length, operating pressure, and mounting configuration.
Do you offer custom cylinders?
Yes. We can help specify and supply custom hydraulic cylinders built to exact requirements.
