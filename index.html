Complete Hydraulic Solutions

Hydraulic Testing Center

Advanced testing benches for pumps, motors, valves, and cylinders fully compliant with ISO 4401 standards.

  • Pump & motor performance testing
  • Valve flow & leakage inspection
  • Cylinder efficiency analysis
  • Contamination reports (ISO 4406)

System Design & Engineering

Custom hydraulic circuit design, manifold block engineering, and power unit assembly by certified experts.

  • ISO 1219 compliant circuit schematics
  • Manifold block configuration
  • HPU design & assembly
  • Electro-hydraulic controls

Training & Certification

Our Process

From Concept to Commissioning

A structured 5-step engineering framework ensuring the highest safety and operational standards.

01

Requirement Analysis

We evaluate load profiles, operating environments, and compliance factors per ISO 4413.

02

Circuit Design & Simulation

Drafting ISO 1219 compliant systems and pre-simulating fluid behavior under pressure.

03

Component Sourcing

Sourcing direct components from trusted brands like ATOS, Chia Wang, Hoyea, and S&S Hydraulics.

04

Assembly & Testing

Cleanroom assembly followed by functional verification and stress testing on certified test benches.

05

Commissioning & Support

On-site installation, team briefing & orientation, and comprehensive warranty coverage.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers regarding technical testing, components, and project delivery schedules.

What hydraulic testing services do you offer?
+
We perform comprehensive tests including pump and motor efficiency metrics, valve leakage checks, cylinder load verification, and fluid particle contamination analysis per ISO 4406.
Which brands do you supply and service?
+
We are authorized distributors for ATOS, Chia Wang, Hoyea, and S&S Hydraulics, while fully capable of servicing components from other top manufacturers upon request.
Can you design custom systems?
+
Yes. Our engineering team handles complete schematic design, hydraulic manifold blocks, and custom power unit assemblies from scratch to finish.
What is the typical turnaround time?
+
Standard component repair and testing takes between 5 to 10 working days. Fast-track emergency protocols are also available for critical cases.
